# Third-party material

The root MIT license covers Super Colour's original code, artwork and prose. It does not relicense third-party material or grant rights to restricted sources.

- Knowledge records in `content/knowledge/` identify sources, attribution, rights status and review status. Consult each record and `content/knowledge/sources/register.yaml` before reusing source-derived passages, translations or values. An `unreviewed` rights status is not permission to reuse.
- Bibliographic records do not grant reproduction rights to the books or other works they describe. Restricted works remain restricted.
- Dependency packages retain their own licenses and notices. Consult the installed packages and the versions pinned in `pnpm-lock.yaml`.
- The site loads DM Sans, DM Mono, Noto Sans JP and Noto Sans SC through Google Fonts. These font families retain their own font licenses; the root MIT license does not apply to font software.

The root license applies only to rights held by the project's contributors. Source attribution and third-party notices must be preserved where their respective terms require it.
